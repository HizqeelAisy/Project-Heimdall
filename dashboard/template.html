      Season so far

      Monthly peak charge is set by the single highest 2pm to 10pm hour. Without the plan, with the plan, and the ringgit kept.

      The evidence, across 99 buildings

      Commercial buildings from the Building Data Genome Project 2, in Austin and Tempe, the closest hot climates in open data. Trained on 2016, tuned on January to March 2017, tested on April to December 2017.

      What the prototype assumes

      • US buildings stand in for Malaysian ones. The model retrains on any building's meter data; the next step is a local pilot.
      • Hourly readings approximate TNB's 30-minute demand intervals.
      • Weather is treated as known a day ahead. Real forecasts carry some error.
      • Managers follow every plan, shifting up to 10% of load into the six hours before. Energy is moved, not removed.
      • Tariff rates are from vendor guides to TNB's July 2025 schedule. TNB states that maximum demand in the off-peak period is not charged.
